## How do I import a question bank?

On the Library tab, tap “Import bank (Excel / CSV)” and pick an .xlsx, .csv, or .tsv file. Single-choice, multiple-choice, true/false, fill-in-the-blank, and short-answer are all supported. First time? Tap “Try sample bank” to explore instantly.

## How does scan-to-search work?

On the Search tab, switch to “Camera”, put the question inside the frame, and the app recognizes it and matches the answer from your imported bank. Recognition and matching run on-device; questions are never uploaded.

## Recognition is off / no match?

Make sure the text is clear, well-lit, and inside the frame; you can also switch to “Text” mode and type keywords. Matching is based on your imported bank — the fuller your bank, the better the hit rate.

## Where are mistakes and favorites?

Wrong answers go to the Mistakes tab automatically; tap the ☆ at the top-right of a question while answering to favorite it.

## Membership & subscription

The free tier has a daily question quota; membership unlocks unlimited questions and AI explanations, offered as a weekly auto-renewable subscription or a one-time lifetime purchase. Subscriptions can be managed or canceled in Settings → Apple Account → Subscriptions.
