<h2>Frequently asked questions</h2>

<h3>How does the AI receipt recognition work?</h3>
<p>It is <strong>bring-your-own-key (BYOK)</strong>. In Settings, choose an AI provider (OpenAI, Anthropic, DeepSeek, Moonshot, Together, or a Custom endpoint) and paste your own API key. Then scan a receipt with the camera, import a screenshot, or use the One-Tap screenshot Shortcut — the app sends the image (or on-device OCR text) directly to your provider to extract amount, merchant, date and category. Your key is stored in the device Keychain and is never sent to us.</p>

<h3>Do I need a subscription to use the app?</h3>
<p>No. Manual expense tracking, statistics, budgets and multiple accounts are free. <strong>Spendlytics Pro</strong> (an auto-renewing subscription) unlocks unlimited AI recognition.</p>

<h3>How do I manage or cancel my subscription?</h3>
<p>Subscriptions are handled by Apple. On your iPhone open <strong>Settings → [your name] → Subscriptions</strong>, select Spendlytics, and change or cancel there. You can also tap <strong>Settings → Spendlytics Pro → Manage subscription</strong> inside the app.</p>

<h3>How do I restore a purchase on a new device?</h3>
<p>Open the paywall in the app and tap <strong>Restore Purchases</strong>, signed in with the same Apple ID used for the original purchase.</p>

<h3>Where is my data stored? How do I delete it?</h3>
<p>Your ledger is stored only on your device. Delete individual records inside the app, or delete the app to remove all of its locally stored data. See our <a href="/en/fernbudget/privacy/">Privacy Policy</a> for details.</p>

<h3>The AI didn't recognize my bill correctly.</h3>
<p>Recognition quality depends on the AI provider/model you configured and the clarity of the image. Try a clearer, full-screen screenshot or a different model. You can always edit any field on the created transaction by hand.</p>
