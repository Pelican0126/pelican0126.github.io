This site used to be a one-page product portfolio. Now it’s a personal site: the home page starts with who I am, and work and notes each have their own page.

## What changed

- There are notes now — you’re reading one. You can subscribe by RSS.
- Work went from a wall of cards to a plain list. The products still have a page each.
- Hosting moved to GitHub Pages. The source is public; there’s a link in the footer.

## What didn’t

- Every old URL still works. The product pages and MoTi’s privacy and support pages are where they were.
- It’s still in Chinese and English; the switch is in the top-right corner.

## How it’s built

The site is written in Astro and builds to plain static pages. The headings are set in LXGW WenKai, and the font files are hosted with the site.

The only script on the page remembers whether you picked light or dark. There are no analytics, no tracking, and no requests to third parties.
